## 1. What we collect

### Your account

- **Identity and contact:** name, email address, phone number.
- **Billing:** company, VAT number (NIPT), address, city and country, which are printed on invoices.
- **Security:** your password (stored only as a one-way hash), the key of your authenticator app (encrypted) and your backup codes (stored only as one-way hashes), the time of your last sign-in, and a record of security events (sign-ins, changes to your account, two-step sign-in, deletion) with the IP address they came from.
- **Sign-in alerts:** a cookie that remembers which browsers you used, so a sign-in from a new one can be emailed to you with its time, IP address and browser.

### What you buy

Orders and invoices (product, plan, amount, dates, payment method and reference), licences, and the servers that activate a licence (an identifier the product sends, its address and the time it last checked in). You pay by card on our payment provider's page; we receive only the result, never the card details.

### PingOmni Monitor

The addresses you monitor and their check results, your status pages, alert channels (such as email addresses or webhook addresses you add), and the people you invite to your workspace.

When a monitor seems down, the address it checks is checked again from PingOmni's own probe servers in other countries and, when none of them answers, from the Globalping network, to confirm the outage before anyone is alerted. Only the address and the kind of check are sent, never your account or other details.

### Status page visitors and subscribers

Our customers publish status pages with PingOmni Monitor, on mon.pingomni.com or on their own domain. Reading a status page, its badge, its widget or its JSON summary sets no cookies and creates no record beyond our web server's logs (see below).

If you **subscribe** to a status page, we store your email address, the language you chose, which page it is for, and when you subscribed and confirmed. We use it only to send you that page's emails: the confirmation link, and messages when a service on the page goes down, recovers or its owner posts an update. Every email has a link to unsubscribe at once. The page's owner (our customer) decides whether the page takes subscribers, can see the list of subscribers' addresses and can remove anyone; for these addresses the page's owner is the controller and PingOmni processes them on its behalf. We never use subscribers' addresses for anything else, and never share them with anyone but our email provider.

### The website and its tools

Our web server records each request (IP address, page, time, browser) in its logs to keep the site working and secure. The free network, DNS and email tools send what you enter (a domain, address or host) to perform the check; some checks are run through the Globalping network or look up public registries (RDAP, PeeringDB, DNS blocklists). We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. The website stores only your choice of light or dark theme and tools view in your browser.

## 3. Who receives it

Only the providers we need, under contract and only for these purposes:

- **Email delivery:** Mailgun, which sends our emails (confirmation, sign-in alerts, invoices, licence notices, Monitor alerts and status page emails to subscribers).
- **Network and security:** Cloudflare, through which our websites are reached.
- **Card payments:** POK (pokpay.io), our payment provider, which receives your card details on its own page and tells us the result.
- **Website tools:** the Globalping network and public registries, for the checks you run (they receive what you enter, not your account).
- **Outage confirmation in PingOmni Monitor:** the Globalping network, when our own probes cannot confirm an outage (it receives the monitored address and the kind of check only).
- **Alert channels you add:** the services you connect (for example Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams, Google Chat, PagerDuty or Telegram) receive the alerts you chose to send them.

Some of these providers may process data outside Albania; where they do, they are bound by contracts with appropriate safeguards. We also disclose data when the law requires it.

## 4. How long we keep it

- **Account data** is kept while the account exists. When you delete your account (Account details → Delete the account), your name, email, phone, company, address and two-step sign-in data are removed at once.
- **Invoices and orders** are kept, with the billing details printed on them, for the period Albanian accounting and tax law requires, even after the account is deleted.
- **Monitor check results** are kept in detail for the history period of your plan; after that only daily totals remain, while the workspace exists.
- **Status page subscriptions** are kept until you unsubscribe, the page's owner removes you, or the page is deleted. A subscription that is never confirmed is deleted after 7 days.
- **Database backups** are kept for 14 days and then overwritten.

## 5. Cookies

PingOmni Account and PingOmni Monitor use only cookies they need to work: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, a security token against forged requests, the browser cookie for sign-in alerts (section 1) and, if you choose "Keep me signed in", a cookie that remembers you. They are not used for advertising or tracking. Public status pages and pingomni.com set no cookies.

## 6. Your rights

You may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. You can correct your details and delete your account yourself in PingOmni Account; for anything else write to hello@pingomni.com. We answer within 30 days.

If you think we use your data unlawfully, you may complain to the Commissioner for the Right to Information and Personal Data Protection (Komisioneri për të Drejtën e Informimit dhe Mbrojtjen e të Dhënave Personale), Tirana, Albania.

## 7. Security

Connections to our services are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords and backup codes are stored as one-way hashes, authenticator keys and email settings encrypted, and access to our servers is restricted. Every new account is asked to set up two-step sign-in; it is required for administrators.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version and its date are always on this page; we tell account holders by email about important changes.
