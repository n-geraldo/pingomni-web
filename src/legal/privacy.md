## 1. What we collect

### Your account

- **Identity and contact:** name, email address, phone number.
- **Billing:** company, VAT number (NIPT), address, city and country, which are printed on invoices.
- **Security:** your password (stored only as a one-way hash), the key of your authenticator app (encrypted) and your backup codes (stored only as one-way hashes), the time of your last sign-in, and a record of security events (sign-ins, changes to your account, two-step sign-in, deletion) with the IP address they came from.
- **Sign-in alerts:** a cookie that remembers which browsers you used, so a sign-in from a new one can be emailed to you with its time, IP address and browser.

### What you buy

Orders and invoices (product, plan, amount, dates, payment method and reference), licences, and the servers that activate a licence (an identifier the product sends, its address and the time it last checked in). When you pay by card, the payment provider handles the card; we receive only the result.

### PingOmni Monitor

The addresses you monitor and their check results, your status pages, alert channels (such as email addresses or webhook addresses you add), and the people you invite to your workspace.

### The website and its tools

Our web server records each request (IP address, page, time, browser) in its logs to keep the site working and secure. The free network, DNS and email tools send what you enter (a domain, address or host) to perform the check; some checks are run through the Globalping network or look up public registries (RDAP, PeeringDB, DNS blocklists). We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. The website stores only your choice of light or dark theme and tools view in your browser.

## 2. Why we use it (purposes and legal bases)

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Creating and running your account, delivering licences and plans, support | Performing our contract with you |
| Invoices, accounting and tax records | Legal obligation |
| Securing accounts: email confirmation, two-step sign-in, sign-in alerts, security records | Our legitimate interest in protecting you and our services, and performing the contract |
| Emails about your orders, licences, renewals and account | Performing the contract |
| Keeping the website and services available and secure (server logs) | Legitimate interest |

We do not sell personal data and we do not send marketing email without your consent.

## 3. Who receives it

Only the providers we need, under contract and only for these purposes:

- **Email delivery:** Mailgun, which sends our emails (confirmation, sign-in alerts, invoices, licence notices).
- **Network and security:** Cloudflare, through which our websites are reached.
- **Card payments:** our payment provider, when card payment is offered.
- **Website tools:** the Globalping network and public registries, for the checks you run (they receive what you enter, not your account).

Some of these providers may process data outside Albania; where they do, they are bound by contracts with appropriate safeguards. We also disclose data when the law requires it.

## 4. How long we keep it

- **Account data** is kept while the account exists. When you delete your account (Account details → Delete the account), your name, email, phone, company, address and two-step sign-in data are removed at once.
- **Invoices and orders** are kept, with the billing details printed on them, for the period Albanian accounting and tax law requires, even after the account is deleted.
- **Monitor check results** are kept in detail for the history period of your plan; after that only daily totals remain, while the workspace exists.
- **Database backups** are kept for 14 days and then overwritten.

## 5. Cookies

PingOmni Account and PingOmni Monitor use only cookies they need to work: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, a security token against forged requests, the browser cookie for sign-in alerts (section 1) and, if you choose "Keep me signed in", a cookie that remembers you. They are not used for advertising or tracking. pingomni.com sets no cookies.

## 6. Your rights

You may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. You can correct your details and delete your account yourself in PingOmni Account; for anything else write to hello@pingomni.com. We answer within 30 days.

If you think we use your data unlawfully, you may complain to the Commissioner for the Right to Information and Personal Data Protection (Komisioneri për të Drejtën e Informimit dhe Mbrojtjen e të Dhënave Personale), Tirana, Albania.

## 7. Security

Connections to our services are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords and backup codes are stored as one-way hashes, authenticator keys and email settings encrypted, and access to our servers is restricted. New accounts must use two-step sign-in.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version and its date are always on this page; we tell account holders by email about important changes.
